# Seal Odyssey - Canon snapshot

Source reviewed: `Seal_Odyssey_Dossier_Maitre_Transmission_Codex_2026-10-01.pdf` (20 pages, dated 2026-10-01).

This note records the parts of the dossier used by the implementation. It is not a replacement for the source document.

## Current canon

- World: Aqualys, broken by the Fracture.
- Metaphysics: the Onde Premiere circulated through the Grand Courant, now split into four Courants Majeurs.
- Current lineages: Soluma, Glaicores, Velmousse, and Sealectes.
- Founding characters: Luma, Nerys, Milo, and Selya.
- Opening slice: Luma reaches the Rivage d'Aelys, explores the Lagune des Murmures, recovers three Echoes, and reactivates an Ancient Site.
- Core promise: attachment to individual seals through needs, personality, preferences, memory, trust, care, and social bonds.
- Conflict language: observation, avoidance, protection, rescue, appeasement, and ecological restoration take priority over destruction.

## Visual guardrails

- Luma remains anatomically a seal: silver-grey body, natural spots, expressive dark eyes, whiskers, and a wet material response.
- No feline, humanoid, armored, or mascot-like body substitutions.
- The target is high-end stylized realism. Any procedural geometry in the web prototype is explicitly a proxy, not a final Luma asset.

## Legacy concepts kept separate

The earlier elemental names Cendrelunes, Nacregivres, Moussecretes, and Brumeciels remain design history. This project does not map them onto or replace the current four lineages without a future explicit decision.

## Open decisions

- Relationship between the current and legacy lineage systems.
- Long-term production engine and migration timing.
- Astronomy, detailed economy, reproduction rules, Voilombre's final ecology/design, the Guardian's visible form, full narrative structure, multiplayer, and monetization.

Code and data must keep these decisions configurable and must not silently canonize an answer.
